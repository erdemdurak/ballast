# Privacy Policy — Hold Focus

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Hold Focus collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no sync, and no analytics.
Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere.

This is not a promise about intent. It is a description of how the app is built: there is
no networking code in it.

## What stays on your device

- **The task you write** and how long you gave it.
- **Your session history** — when a session ran, how many questions were asked, and how
  many times you put the phone down. Kept for 30 days, then reduced to daily counts.
- **Your settings.**

All of it is stored in the app's own container on your iPhone. You can erase every bit of
it at any time: open the app and tap **Erase all history**. There is no confirmation
gauntlet, and nothing is retained afterwards.

Deleting the app removes everything with it.

## Screen Time

If you choose to let Hold Focus notice when a distracting app has been open, iOS asks for
Screen Time permission and shows you Apple's own app picker.

**Hold Focus never learns which apps you picked.** Apple returns opaque tokens — anonymous
handles the app can hand back to the system but cannot read. When the app shows you which
apps interrupted your work, iOS draws those names and icons itself; the app is passing a
token it cannot decode.

Nothing about your app usage is stored beyond a count of interruptions for the session
you are currently running, and that count is erased when the next session starts.

## Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your device. No push server is
involved and no notification content leaves the phone.

## What Hold Focus never does

- It never blocks, restricts, hides or delays access to anything.
- It has no parental mode, no employer mode, and no remote configuration.
- It cannot be pointed at another person's device.
- It shows no ads and contains no third-party SDKs.

## Children

Hold Focus is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.
